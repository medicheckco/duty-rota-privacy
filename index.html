Duty Rota privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Duty Rota keeps everything on your phone.

What the app stores

The company, branch, staff, shift, time-off and rule details you type in. Staff details can include names, staff numbers, jobs, email addresses and phone numbers.

Where it is stored

Only in the app's private storage on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your rota and staff details are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

If you buy Duty Rota Pro, Google Play handles the payment. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro was bought on your Google account; it sends no rota or staff details. Google's privacy policy covers the payment.

When data leaves the phone

Only when you choose to. Sharing a rota (PDF, image or CSV) or saving a backup hands the file to the app you pick, such as email or Google Drive. Those files then follow that app's privacy policy.

Deleting data

Settings → Reset all data removes everything. Uninstalling the app also removes everything. Backups you saved elsewhere are not touched.

Children

The app is for managers planning staff rotas and is not aimed at children.
